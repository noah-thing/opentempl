Liquid chrome 3D lettering prompt for ChatGPT

Updated November 2026

What this style is

A 3D rendered liquid metal lettering: puffy, molten letterforms with mirror chrome material that reflects a pale sky studio, with pinched highlights, rendered on a soft pale ground with a thin interface frame around it.

What makes it look like this

  • puffy molten chrome letters
  • pinched mirror highlights
  • thin rounded HUD frame
  • barcode and QR corner details
  • pale gradient ground

Colours

  • #EAF1F6
  • #9FB6CC
  • #D8C7C2
  • #2B3646
  • #FFFFFF

Silver and pale blue with warm pink reflections on a light ground. For a brand, tint the reflections in the brand colour and keep the metal neutral.

The prompt

Paste this into ChatGPT with image creation on. Change the subject and the three lines of words for your own, and check the spelling before you send it.

Create a finished graphic design: a poster in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format. It is for: Hair studio grand opening.
It must look designed and made by hand by a skilled human designer and illustrator, in the style described below. Treat the style as a strict art direction.

STYLE: Liquid Chrome Type
A 3D rendered liquid metal lettering: puffy, molten letterforms with mirror chrome material that reflects a pale sky studio, with pinched highlights, rendered on a soft pale ground with a thin interface frame around it.
Signature moves to keep:
- puffy molten chrome letters
- pinched mirror highlights
- thin rounded HUD frame
- barcode and QR corner details
- pale gradient ground

SUBJECT
Show Hair studio grand opening in this style. Make the subject the hero and fit everything to it: props, setting and details should belong to this subject, not to the example the style came from.

COMPOSITION
The title fills the centre as one molten mass in two or three lines, with a thin rounded border frame, small circular icons and a barcode in the corners. Rest of the page is quiet pale gradient.

TYPOGRAPHY
The title itself is the artwork, in molten, puffed letters; small clean sans and mono for corners, plus a compass star ornament above.
Render exactly these words, spelled exactly as written, and no other text anywhere:
- headline: "Silver Fox"
- second line: "Colour, cuts and chrome nails"
- small details: "Opening Sat 5 Apr, Odengatan 30"
Give them a clear hierarchy: the headline large and dominant, the rest much smaller.

COLOUR
Palette: #EAF1F6, #9FB6CC, #D8C7C2, #2B3646, #FFFFFF. Silver and pale blue with warm pink reflections on a light ground. For a brand, tint the reflections in the brand colour and keep the metal neutral.

TEXTURE AND FINISH
Mirror reflections with pinched highlights, subtle ground noise, crisp thin frame lines.

AVOID
- flat grey metal
- lens flare streaks
- dark moody lighting
- muddy brown reflections
- gibberish text
- glitter
- anything that looks AI generated: plastic sheen, airbrushed skin, overly smooth gradients, glow and lens flare, HDR, stock photo smiles
- gibberish letters, made up words or any text other than the exact words given
- watermarks, signatures, UI elements, borders or frames around the design
- filler decoration: sparkles, floating dots and stars, confetti, random geometric shapes, gradient orbs, ornamental dividers
- uniform line weight, plastic or airbrushed shading, soft storybook gloss, perfect symmetry, melted or merging details, impossible objects
- showing the design as a mockup (a poster on a wall, a phone, a hand holding it): the artwork itself must fill the whole canvas edge to edge, flat and straight on

Do not search the web or look anything up: work only from this brief, so nothing from elsewhere leaks into the design.
Make the image now, in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format, without asking questions first.

How to use it

  1. Open ChatGPT and check that image creation is available on your account.
  2. Paste the prompt. Replace the subject line and the words in quotes.
  3. Send it. If something is off, ask for one change at a time.
  4. Check every word on the poster. A typo in your prompt is copied into the picture.

Questions

Can ChatGPT make a real liquid chrome 3D lettering print?

It makes an image that imitates the look. For a physical version, use the image as artwork and take a print file to a printer that can reproduce the medium: a 3d rendered liquid metal lettering: puffy, molten letterforms with mirror chrome material that reflects a pale sky studio, with pinched highlights, rendered on a soft pale ground with a thin interface frame around it..

What size should I ask for?

This style is set up for poster (A3 portrait), Instagram post (4:5), story (9:16), square post. Name the format in the prompt, as the examples do, and the layout follows it.

Why do the words come out wrong?

Image models draw letters as pictures, and they sometimes get them wrong. Keep the words short, put them in quotes, and check every letter. In OpenTempl you can leave room for the words and add them as live text.
