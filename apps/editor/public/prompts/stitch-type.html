A cross stitch poster prompt for ChatGPT

Updated November 2026

What this style is

A woven or cross stitched textile pattern built from square stitches, with letters, flowers and ornament drawn on a pixel grid and photographed on fabric or laid over a photograph. Each square has thread texture.

What makes it look like this

  • every letter built from square stitches
  • checkerboard medallion
  • border of repeating flowers
  • thread fibres visible
  • pixel grid alignment

Colours

  • #EFE7D2
  • #7FB04A
  • #E9E34B
  • #E76F51
  • #1B1B1B
  • #3C5A9A

Cream or dark ground with two or three thread colours plus black. For a brand, thread the type in the brand colour and keep the pattern in complementary threads.

The prompt

Paste this into ChatGPT with image creation on. Change the subject and the three lines of words for your own, and check the spelling before you send it.

Create a finished graphic design: a poster in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format. It is for: Monday knitting circle at a community centre.
It must look designed and made by hand by a skilled human designer and illustrator, in the style described below. Treat the style as a strict art direction.

STYLE: Stitched Pixel Type
A woven or cross stitched textile pattern built from square stitches, with letters, flowers and ornament drawn on a pixel grid and photographed on fabric or laid over a photograph. Each square has thread texture.
Signature moves to keep:
- every letter built from square stitches
- checkerboard medallion
- border of repeating flowers
- thread fibres visible
- pixel grid alignment

SUBJECT
Show Monday knitting circle at a community centre in this style. Make the subject the hero and fit everything to it: props, setting and details should belong to this subject, not to the example the style came from.

COMPOSITION
The pixel pattern fills the full sheet as a repeating border or checkerboard medallion, with a title woven as large blocky letters across the middle. Quiet cream rows surround the title for text.

TYPOGRAPHY
Pixel blocky capitals woven from stitches, uniform stroke, set on the grid with no smooth curves.
Render exactly these words, spelled exactly as written, and no other text anywhere:
- headline: "Stitch Night"
- second line: "Bring yarn, we bring tea"
- small details: "Mondays 18:00, Fruängens Bibliotek"
Give them a clear hierarchy: the headline large and dominant, the rest much smaller.

COLOUR
Palette: #EFE7D2, #7FB04A, #E9E34B, #E76F51, #1B1B1B, #3C5A9A. Cream or dark ground with two or three thread colours plus black. For a brand, thread the type in the brand colour and keep the pattern in complementary threads.

TEXTURE AND FINISH
Visible thread twist on each stitch, soft fabric weave, light shadow between stitches.

AVOID
- smooth vector curves
- gradients
- glossy 3D
- photoreal flowers
- lens flare
- gibberish text
- anything that looks AI generated: plastic sheen, airbrushed skin, overly smooth gradients, glow and lens flare, HDR, stock photo smiles
- gibberish letters, made up words or any text other than the exact words given
- watermarks, signatures, UI elements, borders or frames around the design
- filler decoration: sparkles, floating dots and stars, confetti, random geometric shapes, gradient orbs, ornamental dividers
- uniform line weight, plastic or airbrushed shading, soft storybook gloss, perfect symmetry, melted or merging details, impossible objects
- showing the design as a mockup (a poster on a wall, a phone, a hand holding it): the artwork itself must fill the whole canvas edge to edge, flat and straight on

Do not search the web or look anything up: work only from this brief, so nothing from elsewhere leaks into the design.
Make the image now, in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format, without asking questions first.

How to use it

  1. Open ChatGPT and check that image creation is available on your account.
  2. Paste the prompt. Replace the subject line and the words in quotes.
  3. Send it. If something is off, ask for one change at a time.
  4. Check every word on the poster. A typo in your prompt is copied into the picture.

Questions

Can ChatGPT make a real a cross stitch print?

It makes an image that imitates the look. For a physical version, use the image as artwork and take a print file to a printer that can reproduce the medium: a woven or cross stitched textile pattern built from square stitches, with letters, flowers and ornament drawn on a pixel grid and photographed on fabric or laid over a photograph.

What size should I ask for?

This style is set up for poster (A3 portrait), Instagram post (4:5), square post, story (9:16), flyer (A5). Name the format in the prompt, as the examples do, and the layout follows it.

Why do the words come out wrong?

Image models draw letters as pictures, and they sometimes get them wrong. Keep the words short, put them in quotes, and check every letter. In OpenTempl you can leave room for the words and add them as live text.
