A blob character poster prompt for ChatGPT

Updated November 2026

What this style is

A flat vector character illustration with thick clean shapes: oversized black or colour blob figures with huge cartoon eyes, tiny sharp teeth and little flame or sparkle accents, composed with strong scale contrast. It looks like a brand mascot sheet made into a poster.

What makes it look like this

  • giant cropped blob towering
  • huge googly eyes with heavy brows
  • tiny character in an armchair
  • row of mini expressions
  • hashtag title

Colours

  • #141414
  • #F2EFE9
  • #8A7CE8
  • #2BA391
  • #F2564B
  • #FFFFFF

Black or off white ground with a purple mascot, teal clothes and a red flame accent. For a brand, recolour the mascot to the brand colour and keep ground neutral.

The prompt

Paste this into ChatGPT with image creation on. Change the subject and the three lines of words for your own, and check the spelling before you send it.

Create a finished graphic design: a poster in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format. It is for: Children's dentist clinic campaign.
It must look designed and made by hand by a skilled human designer and illustrator, in the style described below. Treat the style as a strict art direction.

STYLE: Blob Mascot
A flat vector character illustration with thick clean shapes: oversized black or colour blob figures with huge cartoon eyes, tiny sharp teeth and little flame or sparkle accents, composed with strong scale contrast. It looks like a brand mascot sheet made into a poster.
Signature moves to keep:
- giant cropped blob towering
- huge googly eyes with heavy brows
- tiny character in an armchair
- row of mini expressions
- hashtag title

SUBJECT
Show Children's dentist clinic campaign in this style. Make the subject the hero and fit everything to it: props, setting and details should belong to this subject, not to the example the style came from.

COMPOSITION
One giant blob fills the top half and is cropped by the edges, staring down, with a small character sitting in an armchair below and a row of three mini characters at the bottom showing different expressions. Clear title or hashtag in the middle.

TYPOGRAPHY
Clean bold sans in white for hashtag or title, tiny pixel or mono text for the line below.
Render exactly these words, spelled exactly as written, and no other text anywhere:
- headline: "Brush Buddies"
- second line: "Tiny monsters hate clean teeth"
- small details: "Free kids check up, Tandkliniken Solna"
Give them a clear hierarchy: the headline large and dominant, the rest much smaller.

COLOUR
Palette: #141414, #F2EFE9, #8A7CE8, #2BA391, #F2564B, #FFFFFF. Black or off white ground with a purple mascot, teal clothes and a red flame accent. For a brand, recolour the mascot to the brand colour and keep ground neutral.

TEXTURE AND FINISH
Flat colour, tiny paper grain, crisp edges.

AVOID
- realistic fur
- gradients
- plastic 3D sheen
- lens flare
- scary gore
- gibberish text
- anything that looks AI generated: plastic sheen, airbrushed skin, overly smooth gradients, glow and lens flare, HDR, stock photo smiles
- gibberish letters, made up words or any text other than the exact words given
- watermarks, signatures, UI elements, borders or frames around the design
- filler decoration: sparkles, floating dots and stars, confetti, random geometric shapes, gradient orbs, ornamental dividers
- uniform line weight, plastic or airbrushed shading, soft storybook gloss, perfect symmetry, melted or merging details, impossible objects
- showing the design as a mockup (a poster on a wall, a phone, a hand holding it): the artwork itself must fill the whole canvas edge to edge, flat and straight on

Do not search the web or look anything up: work only from this brief, so nothing from elsewhere leaks into the design.
Make the image now, in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format, without asking questions first.

How to use it

  1. Open ChatGPT and check that image creation is available on your account.
  2. Paste the prompt. Replace the subject line and the words in quotes.
  3. Send it. If something is off, ask for one change at a time.
  4. Check every word on the poster. A typo in your prompt is copied into the picture.

Questions

Can ChatGPT make a real a blob character print?

It makes an image that imitates the look. For a physical version, use the image as artwork and take a print file to a printer that can reproduce the medium: a flat vector character illustration with thick clean shapes: oversized black or colour blob figures with huge cartoon eyes, tiny sharp teeth and little flame or sparkle accents, composed with strong scale contrast.

What size should I ask for?

This style is set up for poster (A3 portrait), Instagram post (4:5), story (9:16), square post, flyer (A5). Name the format in the prompt, as the examples do, and the layout follows it.

Why do the words come out wrong?

Image models draw letters as pictures, and they sometimes get them wrong. Keep the words short, put them in quotes, and check every letter. In OpenTempl you can leave room for the words and add them as live text.
