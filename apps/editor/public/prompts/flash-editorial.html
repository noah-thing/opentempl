A night out flash photo editorial prompt for ChatGPT

Updated November 2026

What this style is

A direct on camera flash photograph with hard highlights, slightly oversaturated colour and shiny real skin texture, with graphic stickers and acid type layered on top. It reads like a fashion magazine spread from a night out.

What makes it look like this

  • on camera flash look
  • badge shaped stickers cluster
  • oversized outlined letter in a corner
  • registered and copyright symbols as decoration
  • pink and yellow accent

Colours

  • #8EC3F0
  • #F5E614
  • #1B1B1B
  • #F0359A
  • #7A4FE0
  • #FFFFFF

Photo colour is the base; overlays use a hot pink, acid yellow and violet. For a brand, pick one accent from the brand and keep the rest to photo and white.

The prompt

Paste this into ChatGPT with image creation on. Change the subject and the three lines of words for your own, and check the spelling before you send it.

Create a finished graphic design: a poster in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format. It is for: Second hand clothes market in a basement venue.
It must look designed and made by hand by a skilled human designer and illustrator, in the style described below. Treat the style as a strict art direction.

STYLE: Flash Editorial Photo
A direct on camera flash photograph with hard highlights, slightly oversaturated colour and shiny real skin texture, with graphic stickers and acid type layered on top. It reads like a fashion magazine spread from a night out.
Signature moves to keep:
- on camera flash look
- badge shaped stickers cluster
- oversized outlined letter in a corner
- registered and copyright symbols as decoration
- pink and yellow accent

SUBJECT
Show Second hand clothes market in a basement venue in this style. Make the subject the hero and fit everything to it: props, setting and details should belong to this subject, not to the example the style came from.

COMPOSITION
The subject fills the frame in a tight crop, face or garment, with graphic stickers and small badge shapes clustering in the lower half, and one or two huge symbols or letters in the upper left. Quiet sky or wall in the upper right.

TYPOGRAPHY
Oversized outlined bubble capitals, a small mono hashtag line and numbered sticker labels, all in pink, white and black.
Render exactly these words, spelled exactly as written, and no other text anywhere:
- headline: "Thrift Night"
- second line: "Vintage, denim and one night only"
- small details: "Sat 8 Nov, 12 to 20, Fållan, Sofia"
Give them a clear hierarchy: the headline large and dominant, the rest much smaller.

COLOUR
Palette: #8EC3F0, #F5E614, #1B1B1B, #F0359A, #7A4FE0, #FFFFFF. Photo colour is the base; overlays use a hot pink, acid yellow and violet. For a brand, pick one accent from the brand and keep the rest to photo and white.

TEXTURE AND FINISH
Flash hotspot, fine film grain, tiny lens dust, crisp sticker edges with slight shadow.

AVOID
- airbrushed skin
- studio softbox look
- plastic 3D sheen
- lens flare
- symmetrical stock composition
- gibberish text
- anything that looks AI generated: plastic sheen, airbrushed skin, overly smooth gradients, glow and lens flare, HDR, stock photo smiles
- gibberish letters, made up words or any text other than the exact words given
- watermarks, signatures, UI elements, borders or frames around the design
- filler decoration: sparkles, floating dots and stars, confetti, random geometric shapes, gradient orbs, ornamental dividers
- uniform line weight, plastic or airbrushed shading, soft storybook gloss, perfect symmetry, melted or merging details, impossible objects
- showing the design as a mockup (a poster on a wall, a phone, a hand holding it): the artwork itself must fill the whole canvas edge to edge, flat and straight on

Do not search the web or look anything up: work only from this brief, so nothing from elsewhere leaks into the design.
Make the image now, in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format, without asking questions first.

How to use it

  1. Open ChatGPT and check that image creation is available on your account.
  2. Paste the prompt. Replace the subject line and the words in quotes.
  3. Send it. If something is off, ask for one change at a time.
  4. Check every word on the poster. A typo in your prompt is copied into the picture.

Questions

Can ChatGPT make a real a night out flash photo editorial print?

It makes an image that imitates the look. For a physical version, use the image as artwork and take a print file to a printer that can reproduce the medium: a direct on camera flash photograph with hard highlights, slightly oversaturated colour and shiny real skin texture, with graphic stickers and acid type layered on top.

What size should I ask for?

This style is set up for poster (A3 portrait), Instagram post (4:5), story (9:16), square post. Name the format in the prompt, as the examples do, and the layout follows it.

Why do the words come out wrong?

Image models draw letters as pictures, and they sometimes get them wrong. Keep the words short, put them in quotes, and check every letter. In OpenTempl you can leave room for the words and add them as live text.
