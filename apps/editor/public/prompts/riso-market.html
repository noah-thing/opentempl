A multi ink risograph illustration prompt for ChatGPT

Updated November 2026

What this style is

A multi ink risograph illustration where each colour is a separate flat layer with a grainy tone, so overlapping shapes make new colours. People and stalls are simplified into chunky overlapping shapes.

What makes it look like this

  • translucent ink overlaps
  • figures with stripes and dots patterns
  • market awning at the edge
  • letters in different inks
  • tiny squiggles and sparkles

Colours

  • #FFFDF6
  • #FF6FA8
  • #FFD83A
  • #26B6E8
  • #3AA757
  • #F0592E

Light paper with pink, yellow, sky blue, green and an orange red accent, all as translucent inks. For a brand, pick four inks including the brand colour and let overlaps do the mixing.

The prompt

Paste this into ChatGPT with image creation on. Change the subject and the three lines of words for your own, and check the spelling before you send it.

Create a finished graphic design: a poster in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format. It is for: Autumn harvest fair at a community garden.
It must look designed and made by hand by a skilled human designer and illustrator, in the style described below. Treat the style as a strict art direction.

STYLE: Riso Market Scene
A multi ink risograph illustration where each colour is a separate flat layer with a grainy tone, so overlapping shapes make new colours. People and stalls are simplified into chunky overlapping shapes.
Signature moves to keep:
- translucent ink overlaps
- figures with stripes and dots patterns
- market awning at the edge
- letters in different inks
- tiny squiggles and sparkles

SUBJECT
Show Autumn harvest fair at a community garden in this style. Make the subject the hero and fit everything to it: props, setting and details should belong to this subject, not to the example the style came from.

COMPOSITION
A busy scene crowds the full page, with the title tucked top left in the sky of the scene and the info line in pink along the bottom. A striped awning at the top corner, figures diagonal across the middle and a table of produce lower left.

TYPOGRAPHY
Chunky hand cut capitals each letter in a different ink, stacked in three lines, and a small wide spaced caps line for info.
Render exactly these words, spelled exactly as written, and no other text anywhere:
- headline: "Harvest Fair"
- second line: "Pickles, apples, honey and a swap table"
- small details: "Sat 4 Oct, 10 to 15, Kolonilotten Tallkrogen"
Give them a clear hierarchy: the headline large and dominant, the rest much smaller.

COLOUR
Palette: #FFFDF6, #FF6FA8, #FFD83A, #26B6E8, #3AA757, #F0592E. Light paper with pink, yellow, sky blue, green and an orange red accent, all as translucent inks. For a brand, pick four inks including the brand colour and let overlaps do the mixing.

TEXTURE AND FINISH
Visible riso grain, ink overprints, tiny gaps between inks, paper texture.

AVOID
- outlines in black
- gradients
- photorealism
- plastic 3D sheen
- lens flare
- gibberish text
- anything that looks AI generated: plastic sheen, airbrushed skin, overly smooth gradients, glow and lens flare, HDR, stock photo smiles
- gibberish letters, made up words or any text other than the exact words given
- watermarks, signatures, UI elements, borders or frames around the design
- filler decoration: sparkles, floating dots and stars, confetti, random geometric shapes, gradient orbs, ornamental dividers
- uniform line weight, plastic or airbrushed shading, soft storybook gloss, perfect symmetry, melted or merging details, impossible objects
- showing the design as a mockup (a poster on a wall, a phone, a hand holding it): the artwork itself must fill the whole canvas edge to edge, flat and straight on

Do not search the web or look anything up: work only from this brief, so nothing from elsewhere leaks into the design.

How to use it

  1. Open ChatGPT and check that image creation is available on your account.
  2. Paste the prompt. Replace the subject line and the words in quotes.
  3. Send it. If something is off, ask for one change at a time.
  4. Check every word on the poster. A typo in your prompt is copied into the picture.

Questions

Can ChatGPT make a real a multi ink risograph illustration print?

It makes an image that imitates the look. For a physical version, use the image as artwork and take a print file to a printer that can reproduce the medium: a multi ink risograph illustration where each colour is a separate flat layer with a grainy tone, so overlapping shapes make new colours.

What size should I ask for?

This style is set up for poster (A3 portrait), Instagram post (4:5), square post, story (9:16), flyer (A5). Name the format in the prompt, as the examples do, and the layout follows it.

Why do the words come out wrong?

Image models draw letters as pictures, and they sometimes get them wrong. Keep the words short, put them in quotes, and check every letter. In OpenTempl you can leave room for the words and add them as live text.
