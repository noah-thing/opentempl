A cut paper collage poster prompt for ChatGPT

Updated November 2026

What this style is

A bright mixed media collage of hard cut photographs, coloured paper shapes, scribbled vector doodles and tape, assembled digitally to look like cut paper. Photos are slightly desaturated and the paper is saturated.

What makes it look like this

  • starburst behind a cut figure
  • photo inside the body shape
  • torn blue tape corners
  • big yellow sun circle
  • scribbled doodles on top

Colours

  • #E8271F
  • #F7C41B
  • #F2698F
  • #2D53A8
  • #2E8B4A
  • #1E1B18

Saturated paper colours against slightly muted photos, with one red burst as the loudest shape. For a brand, make the burst the brand main colour and use two supporting paper colours.

The prompt

Paste this into ChatGPT with image creation on. Change the subject and the three lines of words for your own, and check the spelling before you send it.

Create a finished graphic design: a poster in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format. It is for: High school spring musical.
It must look designed and made by hand by a skilled human designer and illustrator, in the style described below. Treat the style as a strict art direction.

STYLE: Pop Cutout Collage
A bright mixed media collage of hard cut photographs, coloured paper shapes, scribbled vector doodles and tape, assembled digitally to look like cut paper. Photos are slightly desaturated and the paper is saturated.
Signature moves to keep:
- starburst behind a cut figure
- photo inside the body shape
- torn blue tape corners
- big yellow sun circle
- scribbled doodles on top

SUBJECT
Show High school spring musical in this style. Make the subject the hero and fit everything to it: props, setting and details should belong to this subject, not to the example the style came from.

COMPOSITION
A cut out figure stands centred on a big starburst shape, with a photograph of a different scale breaking through its body, such as flowers across the legs. Coloured paper blocks and torn tape pieces frame the edges and the title sits in a clean band.

TYPOGRAPHY
Bold blocky grotesk in cream or black on cut paper strips, plus hand scribbled marker words in neon lines.
Render exactly these words, spelled exactly as written, and no other text anywhere:
- headline: "Grease Lightning"
- second line: "Spring musical by Year 12 drama"
- small details: "24 to 26 April, 18:30, Aula Norra"
Give them a clear hierarchy: the headline large and dominant, the rest much smaller.

COLOUR
Palette: #E8271F, #F7C41B, #F2698F, #2D53A8, #2E8B4A, #1E1B18. Saturated paper colours against slightly muted photos, with one red burst as the loudest shape. For a brand, make the burst the brand main colour and use two supporting paper colours.

TEXTURE AND FINISH
Paper fibre, torn tape, subtle shadows, small halftone speckle on photos.

AVOID
- perfect vector gradients
- plastic 3D sheen
- lens flare
- unrealistic skin smoothing
- gibberish text
- anything that looks AI generated: plastic sheen, airbrushed skin, overly smooth gradients, glow and lens flare, HDR, stock photo smiles
- gibberish letters, made up words or any text other than the exact words given
- watermarks, signatures, UI elements, borders or frames around the design
- filler decoration: sparkles, floating dots and stars, confetti, random geometric shapes, gradient orbs, ornamental dividers
- uniform line weight, plastic or airbrushed shading, soft storybook gloss, perfect symmetry, melted or merging details, impossible objects
- showing the design as a mockup (a poster on a wall, a phone, a hand holding it): the artwork itself must fill the whole canvas edge to edge, flat and straight on

Do not search the web or look anything up: work only from this brief, so nothing from elsewhere leaks into the design.
Make the image now, in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format, without asking questions first.

How to use it

  1. Open ChatGPT and check that image creation is available on your account.
  2. Paste the prompt. Replace the subject line and the words in quotes.
  3. Send it. If something is off, ask for one change at a time.
  4. Check every word on the poster. A typo in your prompt is copied into the picture.

Questions

Can ChatGPT make a real a cut paper collage print?

It makes an image that imitates the look. For a physical version, use the image as artwork and take a print file to a printer that can reproduce the medium: a bright mixed media collage of hard cut photographs, coloured paper shapes, scribbled vector doodles and tape, assembled digitally to look like cut paper.

What size should I ask for?

This style is set up for poster (A3 portrait), Instagram post (4:5), story (9:16), square post, flyer (A5). Name the format in the prompt, as the examples do, and the layout follows it.

Why do the words come out wrong?

Image models draw letters as pictures, and they sometimes get them wrong. Keep the words short, put them in quotes, and check every letter. In OpenTempl you can leave room for the words and add them as live text.
