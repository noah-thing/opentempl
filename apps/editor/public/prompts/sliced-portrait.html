A cut portrait cover prompt for ChatGPT

Updated November 2026

What this style is

A photographic cover where a studio portrait is physically or digitally manipulated: concentric cuts, strips, torn paper or huge type masking parts of the face. The manipulation looks like cut paper or a layered print, not a filter.

What makes it look like this

  • concentric cut mouth or eyes
  • torn strips shifting the face
  • masthead partly covered by the head
  • single red issue number
  • small barcode

Colours

  • #EFEAE0
  • #161616
  • #E5352A
  • #D9C9A8

Mostly neutral photo tones with black type and one red accent. For a brand, use the brand colour for the single accent and the masthead.

The prompt

Paste this into ChatGPT with image creation on. Change the subject and the three lines of words for your own, and check the spelling before you send it.

Create a finished graphic design: a poster in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format. It is for: Independent magazine about neighbourhood people.
It must look designed and made by hand by a skilled human designer and illustrator, in the style described below. Treat the style as a strict art direction.

STYLE: Sliced Portrait Cover
A photographic cover where a studio portrait is physically or digitally manipulated: concentric cuts, strips, torn paper or huge type masking parts of the face. The manipulation looks like cut paper or a layered print, not a filter.
Signature moves to keep:
- concentric cut mouth or eyes
- torn strips shifting the face
- masthead partly covered by the head
- single red issue number
- small barcode

SUBJECT
Show Independent magazine about neighbourhood people in this style. Make the subject the hero and fit everything to it: props, setting and details should belong to this subject, not to the example the style came from.

COMPOSITION
Masthead across the top in a big serif or grotesk, the face occupying the whole sheet with a visible cut pattern in the middle, one or two cover lines lower left or right in small type, and a small red issue number. The rest of the face is left untouched so the cut reads clearly.

TYPOGRAPHY
Large refined serif masthead, and small tidy sans cover lines in black, with a single red numeral.
Render exactly these words, spelled exactly as written, and no other text anywhere:
- headline: "Kvarter"
- second line: "Issue 05: the faces of Skanstull"
- small details: "Out now at Pressbyrån and kvarter.se"
Give them a clear hierarchy: the headline large and dominant, the rest much smaller.

COLOUR
Palette: #EFEAE0, #161616, #E5352A, #D9C9A8. Mostly neutral photo tones with black type and one red accent. For a brand, use the brand colour for the single accent and the masthead.

TEXTURE AND FINISH
Fine photographic grain, cut paper edges with soft shadow, slight paper creases.

AVOID
- airbrushed skin
- plastic 3D sheen
- heavy filters
- lens flare
- gibberish text
- gore
- anything that looks AI generated: plastic sheen, airbrushed skin, overly smooth gradients, glow and lens flare, HDR, stock photo smiles
- gibberish letters, made up words or any text other than the exact words given
- watermarks, signatures, UI elements, borders or frames around the design
- filler decoration: sparkles, floating dots and stars, confetti, random geometric shapes, gradient orbs, ornamental dividers
- uniform line weight, plastic or airbrushed shading, soft storybook gloss, perfect symmetry, melted or merging details, impossible objects
- showing the design as a mockup (a poster on a wall, a phone, a hand holding it): the artwork itself must fill the whole canvas edge to edge, flat and straight on

Do not search the web or look anything up: work only from this brief, so nothing from elsewhere leaks into the design.
Make the image now, in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format, without asking questions first.

How to use it

  1. Open ChatGPT and check that image creation is available on your account.
  2. Paste the prompt. Replace the subject line and the words in quotes.
  3. Send it. If something is off, ask for one change at a time.
  4. Check every word on the poster. A typo in your prompt is copied into the picture.

Questions

Can ChatGPT make a real a cut portrait cover print?

It makes an image that imitates the look. For a physical version, use the image as artwork and take a print file to a printer that can reproduce the medium: a photographic cover where a studio portrait is physically or digitally manipulated: concentric cuts, strips, torn paper or huge type masking parts of the face.

What size should I ask for?

This style is set up for poster (A3 portrait), Instagram post (4:5), story (9:16), flyer (A5). Name the format in the prompt, as the examples do, and the layout follows it.

Why do the words come out wrong?

Image models draw letters as pictures, and they sometimes get them wrong. Keep the words short, put them in quotes, and check every letter. In OpenTempl you can leave room for the words and add them as live text.
