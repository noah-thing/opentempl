A smeared photocopy rave flyer prompt for ChatGPT

Updated November 2026

What this style is

A photocopied rave flyer smeared on purpose: the paper dragged during copying so fluorescent ink type streaks sideways, black stock, heavy grain.

What makes it look like this

  • melting warped headline
  • coarse noise grain
  • single heat colour glow
  • small sharp lineup text
  • dark negative space

Colours

  • #140A0A
  • #E8200F
  • #FF6A1A
  • #FFE14D
  • #2A6BFF

A black ground with one heat colour and its glow, or a three colour rainbow warp for daytime. For a brand, use the brand hot colour as the glow and the darkest colour as ground.

The prompt

Paste this into ChatGPT with image creation on. Change the subject and the three lines of words for your own, and check the spelling before you send it.

Create a finished graphic design: a poster in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format. It is for: Sunday night bass music club night.
It must look designed and made by hand by a skilled human designer and illustrator, in the style described below. Treat the style as a strict art direction.

STYLE: Grain Glow Type
A photocopied rave flyer smeared on purpose: the paper dragged during copying so fluorescent ink type streaks sideways, black stock, heavy grain.
Signature moves to keep:
- melting warped headline
- coarse noise grain
- single heat colour glow
- small sharp lineup text
- dark negative space

SUBJECT
Show Sunday night bass music club night in this style. Make the subject the hero and fit everything to it: props, setting and details should belong to this subject, not to the example the style came from.

COMPOSITION
One huge date or word bleeds into the centre of the frame, warped like it is melting, with a small cluster of lineup lines sharp enough to read set left. A bloom shape glows in the lower right corner. Abundant dark space.

TYPOGRAPHY
Soft blurred heavy grotesk or rounded type for the headline, with sharp small condensed type for details. Warp, mirror and ripple effects on one word only.
Render exactly these words, spelled exactly as written, and no other text anywhere:
- headline: "Bassline Sundays"
- second line: "Resident DJs Nova, Kiri and Tuff"
- small details: "Sun 22:00 to 04:00, Klubb Vagn, Malmö"
Give them a clear hierarchy: the headline large and dominant, the rest much smaller.

COLOUR
Palette: #140A0A, #E8200F, #FF6A1A, #FFE14D, #2A6BFF. A black ground with one heat colour and its glow, or a three colour rainbow warp for daytime. For a brand, use the brand hot colour as the glow and the darkest colour as ground.

TEXTURE AND FINISH
Dense film grain, noise, blooming halation, no sharp edges on the headline.

AVOID
- neon glow or bloom
- blurry soft focus type
- lens effects
- clean vector type
- drop shadow
- 3D bevel
- lens flare streaks
- gibberish lineup names
- anything that looks AI generated: plastic sheen, airbrushed skin, overly smooth gradients, glow and lens flare, HDR, stock photo smiles
- gibberish letters, made up words or any text other than the exact words given
- watermarks, signatures, UI elements, borders or frames around the design
- filler decoration: sparkles, floating dots and stars, confetti, random geometric shapes, gradient orbs, ornamental dividers
- uniform line weight, plastic or airbrushed shading, soft storybook gloss, perfect symmetry, melted or merging details, impossible objects
- showing the design as a mockup (a poster on a wall, a phone, a hand holding it): the artwork itself must fill the whole canvas edge to edge, flat and straight on

Do not search the web or look anything up: work only from this brief, so nothing from elsewhere leaks into the design.

How to use it

  1. Open ChatGPT and check that image creation is available on your account.
  2. Paste the prompt. Replace the subject line and the words in quotes.
  3. Send it. If something is off, ask for one change at a time.
  4. Check every word on the poster. A typo in your prompt is copied into the picture.

Questions

Can ChatGPT make a real a smeared photocopy rave flyer print?

It makes an image that imitates the look. For a physical version, use the image as artwork and take a print file to a printer that can reproduce the medium: a photocopied rave flyer smeared on purpose: the paper dragged during copying so fluorescent ink type streaks sideways, black stock, heavy grain..

What size should I ask for?

This style is set up for poster (A3 portrait), Instagram post (4:5), story (9:16), square post, flyer (A5). Name the format in the prompt, as the examples do, and the layout follows it.

Why do the words come out wrong?

Image models draw letters as pictures, and they sometimes get them wrong. Keep the words short, put them in quotes, and check every letter. In OpenTempl you can leave room for the words and add them as live text.
