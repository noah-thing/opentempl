A grainy gradient animal illustration prompt for ChatGPT

Updated November 2026

What this style is

A digital and gouache illustration where an animal is assembled from bold geometric slabs, each filled with a soft speckled airbrush gradient that fades two colours together. The grain looks like sprayed ink on paper.

What makes it look like this

  • animal made of slabs and wedges
  • grain gradient between two colours per shape
  • tiny house row at the bottom
  • small star and bird companions
  • type tilted with the artwork

Colours

  • #EAB308
  • #E5121E
  • #2B3FA8
  • #1E7A3A
  • #F05A9E
  • #2B1B16

Mustard yellow ground with primary red, blue and green shapes and one pink accent. Brand adaptation: keep the warm ground and recolour the shapes in the brand pair, letting grain fade each into the other.

The prompt

Paste this into ChatGPT with image creation on. Change the subject and the three lines of words for your own, and check the spelling before you send it.

Create a finished graphic design: a poster in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format. It is for: Children's theatre weekend at the town library.
It must look designed and made by hand by a skilled human designer and illustrator, in the style described below. Treat the style as a strict art direction.

STYLE: Grain Shape Animals
A digital and gouache illustration where an animal is assembled from bold geometric slabs, each filled with a soft speckled airbrush gradient that fades two colours together. The grain looks like sprayed ink on paper.
Signature moves to keep:
- animal made of slabs and wedges
- grain gradient between two colours per shape
- tiny house row at the bottom
- small star and bird companions
- type tilted with the artwork

SUBJECT
Show Children's theatre weekend at the town library in this style. Make the subject the hero and fit everything to it: props, setting and details should belong to this subject, not to the example the style came from.

COMPOSITION
One large animal leans diagonally across most of the page, with small companions like a bird or clouds in the open yellow. A row of tiny houses or hills can run along the bottom. Text sits in the clear lower right and along the vertical edge.

TYPOGRAPHY
Clean geometric sans in mixed colours, set at an angle that follows the animal, with colour changes between lines. Small vertical credit text in the margin.
Render exactly these words, spelled exactly as written, and no other text anywhere:
- headline: "Little Stage Weekend"
- second line: "Puppets, stories and a surprise orchestra"
- small details: "12 to 13 April, Stadsbiblioteket, free entry"
Give them a clear hierarchy: the headline large and dominant, the rest much smaller.

COLOUR
Palette: #EAB308, #E5121E, #2B3FA8, #1E7A3A, #F05A9E, #2B1B16. Mustard yellow ground with primary red, blue and green shapes and one pink accent. Brand adaptation: keep the warm ground and recolour the shapes in the brand pair, letting grain fade each into the other.

TEXTURE AND FINISH
Spray grain stipple inside each shape, edges crisp, flat colour elsewhere.

AVOID
- cute realistic fur
- outlines
- plastic 3D sheen
- stock clipart
- gibberish text
- lens flare
- anything that looks AI generated: plastic sheen, airbrushed skin, overly smooth gradients, glow and lens flare, HDR, stock photo smiles
- gibberish letters, made up words or any text other than the exact words given
- watermarks, signatures, UI elements, borders or frames around the design
- filler decoration: sparkles, floating dots and stars, confetti, random geometric shapes, gradient orbs, ornamental dividers
- uniform line weight, plastic or airbrushed shading, soft storybook gloss, perfect symmetry, melted or merging details, impossible objects
- showing the design as a mockup (a poster on a wall, a phone, a hand holding it): the artwork itself must fill the whole canvas edge to edge, flat and straight on

Do not search the web or look anything up: work only from this brief, so nothing from elsewhere leaks into the design.

How to use it

  1. Open ChatGPT and check that image creation is available on your account.
  2. Paste the prompt. Replace the subject line and the words in quotes.
  3. Send it. If something is off, ask for one change at a time.
  4. Check every word on the poster. A typo in your prompt is copied into the picture.

Questions

Can ChatGPT make a real a grainy gradient animal illustration print?

It makes an image that imitates the look. For a physical version, use the image as artwork and take a print file to a printer that can reproduce the medium: a digital and gouache illustration where an animal is assembled from bold geometric slabs, each filled with a soft speckled airbrush gradient that fades two colours together.

What size should I ask for?

This style is set up for poster (A3 portrait), Instagram post (4:5), story (9:16), square post, flyer (A5). Name the format in the prompt, as the examples do, and the layout follows it.

Why do the words come out wrong?

Image models draw letters as pictures, and they sometimes get them wrong. Keep the words short, put them in quotes, and check every letter. In OpenTempl you can leave room for the words and add them as live text.
