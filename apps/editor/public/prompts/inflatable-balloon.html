Balloon lettering prompt for ChatGPT

Updated November 2026

What this style is

A 3D rendered or photographed inflatable look: letters and creatures made of balloon skin or vinyl, with soft reflections, pinch points at the ends and a thin outline of contrast colour. Believable lighting and tiny imperfections.

What makes it look like this

  • puffy balloon material with pinch points
  • thick coloured sticker outline
  • letters inside circles
  • bright flat ground
  • visible seams and wrinkles

Colours

  • #F6F6F8
  • #E64A2E
  • #5EC2F5
  • #FFE16A
  • #FF4FA3

White or pearly inflatable on a bright flat ground with a thick contrast outline. For a brand, use the brand colour as the ground and keep the balloon neutral, or invert them.

The prompt

Paste this into ChatGPT with image creation on. Change the subject and the three lines of words for your own, and check the spelling before you send it.

Create a finished graphic design: a poster in A3 portrait (1:1.41) format. It is for: Bouncy castle rental for children's parties.
It must look designed and made by hand by a skilled human designer and illustrator, in the style described below. Treat the style as a strict art direction.

STYLE: Inflatable Type
A 3D rendered or photographed inflatable look: letters and creatures made of balloon skin or vinyl, with soft reflections, pinch points at the ends and a thin outline of contrast colour. Believable lighting and tiny imperfections.
Signature moves to keep:
- puffy balloon material with pinch points
- thick coloured sticker outline
- letters inside circles
- bright flat ground
- visible seams and wrinkles

SUBJECT
Show Bouncy castle rental for children's parties in this style. Make the subject the hero and fit everything to it: props, setting and details should belong to this subject, not to the example the style came from.

COMPOSITION
One big inflatable subject fills most of the frame, cropped slightly, with a thick sticker outline in a contrasting colour. A cluster of text circles or dots sits upper left and details in the bottom band.

TYPOGRAPHY
Typewriter or round mono letters set inside yellow dots, or puffy 3D letters in the same material. Small justified serif details.
Render exactly these words, spelled exactly as written, and no other text anywhere:
- headline: "Hoppa Hela Sommaren"
- second line: "Castles, slides and balloon arches for your party"
- small details: "Book now at hoppa.example, 070 123 45 67"
Give them a clear hierarchy: the headline large and dominant, the rest much smaller.

COLOUR
Palette: #F6F6F8, #E64A2E, #5EC2F5, #FFE16A, #FF4FA3. White or pearly inflatable on a bright flat ground with a thick contrast outline. For a brand, use the brand colour as the ground and keep the balloon neutral, or invert them.

TEXTURE AND FINISH
Rubber skin, subtle seams, finger smudges, soft studio reflections, a little dust.

AVOID
- perfectly plastic CGI gloss
- lens flare
- chrome unless asked
- gibberish text
- creepy faces
- dark moody lighting
- anything that looks AI generated: plastic sheen, airbrushed skin, overly smooth gradients, glow and lens flare, HDR, stock photo smiles
- gibberish letters, made up words or any text other than the exact words given
- watermarks, signatures, UI elements, borders or frames around the design
- filler decoration: sparkles, floating dots and stars, confetti, random geometric shapes, gradient orbs, ornamental dividers
- uniform line weight, plastic or airbrushed shading, soft storybook gloss, perfect symmetry, melted or merging details, impossible objects
- showing the design as a mockup (a poster on a wall, a phone, a hand holding it): the artwork itself must fill the whole canvas edge to edge, flat and straight on

Do not search the web or look anything up: work only from this brief, so nothing from elsewhere leaks into the design.

How to use it

  1. Open ChatGPT and check that image creation is available on your account.
  2. Paste the prompt. Replace the subject line and the words in quotes.
  3. Send it. If something is off, ask for one change at a time.
  4. Check every word on the poster. A typo in your prompt is copied into the picture.

Questions

Can ChatGPT make a real balloon lettering print?

It makes an image that imitates the look. For a physical version, use the image as artwork and take a print file to a printer that can reproduce the medium: a 3d rendered or photographed inflatable look: letters and creatures made of balloon skin or vinyl, with soft reflections, pinch points at the ends and a thin outline of contrast colour.

What size should I ask for?

This style is set up for poster (A3 portrait), Instagram post (4:5), story (9:16), square post, flyer (A5). Name the format in the prompt, as the examples do, and the layout follows it.

Why do the words come out wrong?

Image models draw letters as pictures, and they sometimes get them wrong. Keep the words short, put them in quotes, and check every letter. In OpenTempl you can leave room for the words and add them as live text.
