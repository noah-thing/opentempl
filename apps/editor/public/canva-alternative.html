A Canva alternative you can keep, offline and free

OpenTempl is a free, open source design app for posters, social posts, slides, menus and invitations. It works offline, needs no account and has no paid tier. It is not affiliated with Canva.

Updated November 2026

If you are looking for a Canva alternative, the useful question is what you use Canva for. For making your own posters, posts and slides, OpenTempl does the job and you keep the files. For team workspaces, huge stock libraries and phone apps, it does not, and Canva is the better tool.

Who should switch

Switch if you:

  • make posters, Instagram posts, flyers, menus or slides for yourself, a small business, a club or a class
  • want to open the app without logging in
  • work on a train, in a classroom with poor wifi or on a computer that blocks sign ups
  • want transparent PNG, SVG or print PDF without paying
  • would rather have a file you own than a design stored in someone else's account
  • like templates with a strong visual style, not only the clean corporate kind

Stay with Canva if you:

  • share folders and brand approvals with a team
  • need millions of stock photos, videos and music tracks
  • design mostly on your phone
  • rely on Canva's AI writing or scheduling tools

What you get

  • Templates. 337 across 34 styles, from Swiss grid and riso zine to brutalist, Bauhaus and Y2K. Browse them.
  • A real editor. Pages, smart guides that show the spacing, 23 shapes, a vector pen, charts, tables, QR codes, curved text, 1,344 Google Fonts and your own font files.
  • Photo tools. Crop, masks, filters, adjustment sliders and background removal that runs on your computer.
  • Resize and repeat. Magic resize into any format, bulk create from a pasted table, brand kits.
  • Import. PowerPoint and PDF files, with editable text.
  • Export. PNG including transparent, JPG, WebP, SVG, PDF for screen or print, PowerPoint, MP4 and GIF.
  • Live editing. Share a link and edit together, with each other's cursors and selections.
  • Images from your own OpenAI key. 32 styles in the Generate tab. OpenAI bills you.

Is it better than Canva?

It depends on the job. OpenTempl is better in these ways:

  1. It works offline from the first minute and creates new designs offline. Canva's offline mode edits designs you marked ahead of time, for a limited period.
  2. You do not need an account.
  3. The files are yours, in an editable OpenTempl file and in open formats.
  4. Exports that Canva keeps behind a paid plan, such as transparent PNG and SVG, are free. See Canva Pro features in OpenTempl.
  5. It is open source.

Canva is better in these ways:

  1. The template and stock library is far larger.
  2. It has phone apps.
  3. It has team workspaces, comments, approvals and brand controls at company scale.
  4. It has AI tools and a content planner built in.
  5. It has years of polish and a very large user base. OpenTempl is at version 0.1.

Moving your Canva work over

In Canva, download a design as PDF or PowerPoint. Open the file in OpenTempl. PowerPoint files keep their text, shapes, pictures and notes. PDF text becomes editable text boxes. Fonts that are not available get a close match, and complex effects can flatten, so check the result before you rely on it. There is no direct Canva import.

Questions

Is OpenTempl a Canva clone?

No. It is a separate editor written from scratch, with its own templates and its own look. It does not use Canva's code, content or servers.

Is OpenTempl better than Canva?

For working offline, owning your files and exporting without a paid plan, yes. For templates and stock at scale, phone apps and team features, no. The lists above give both sides.

Can I import Canva designs?

Download the design from Canva as PDF or PowerPoint and open that file in OpenTempl. There is no direct Canva import.

Is there a catch?

It is a young project by one maker. There is no cloud sync and no phone app, and the desktop installers are not code signed yet. The Generate tab uses your own OpenAI account, which costs money for each image.

Which Canva Pro features are free in OpenTempl?

Background removal, magic resize, brand kits, bulk create, transparent PNG, SVG export, print PDF with bleed, version history and custom fonts. See the list.
