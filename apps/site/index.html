#06 · Together

Send a link and edit at the same time. You see where everyone is pointing and what they have selected, comments sit right on the page, and every change goes straight from one browser to the other.

a live session, seen from the second laptop

#07 · Privacy

It watches your cursor.
Nothing else.

There is no account to make and nothing gets uploaded. Designs live in your browser on this machine. When you design with a friend, every change travels straight between your two browsers, encrypted with a key that only the link holds.

Want a copy for email or a backup drive? Export a .tavla file. It holds every page, photo and choice you made.

#08 · Open source

Read every line.

Tavla is MIT licensed. React and TypeScript for the editor, Yjs for undo, offline saving and live sessions. Fork it, theme it, put it in your school.
