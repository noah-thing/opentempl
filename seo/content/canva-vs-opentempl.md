Canva is the large, polished default for design on the web. OpenTempl is a small, free, open source app that works offline. This page sets them side by side, including where Canva is better. OpenTempl is not affiliated with Canva. The Canva details come from its help and pricing pages as read in October 2026; check canva.com for today's terms.

## Side by side

| | Canva | OpenTempl |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Free plan, paid plans for more tools and exports | Free, no paid plan |
| Licence | Proprietary | MIT, open source |
| Account | Needed | Not needed |
| Works offline | Edit designs you marked in advance, for a limited time | Yes, and new designs too |
| Where your files live | In your Canva account | On your device, plus an editable file you keep |
| Templates | A very large library | {{templates}} in {{vibes}} styles |
| Stock photos, video, music | Very large library, some behind a paid plan | Built in photos and objects, plus Openverse and Iconify search |
| Transparent PNG, SVG, print PDF | Paid plan | Free |
| Background remover, magic resize, bulk create | Paid plan | Free |
| AI tools | Built in, metered | Optional Generate tab with your own OpenAI key |
| Import | Several formats | PowerPoint and PDF, with editable text |
| Real time editing with others | Yes, with accounts and team features | Yes, from a link, peer to peer |
| Team features | Workspaces, approvals, brand controls | None |
| Phone apps | Yes | No |
| Publishing and scheduling | Yes | No |
| CMYK for print | On paid plans | Not yet |

## Pick Canva if

1. You work in a team that shares folders, comments and approvals.
2. You need a huge stock library, or video and music tracks.
3. You make designs on your phone.
4. You want built in AI writing and a content planner.
5. You want the most polished, most documented tool, with years behind it.

## Pick OpenTempl if

1. You want to open an app and start, with no account.
2. You work offline, or on a computer that blocks sign ups.
3. You want transparent PNG, SVG and print PDF without a plan.
4. You want files you own, in a format you can keep and open source code behind it.
5. You like templates with a clear visual style: Swiss grid, riso, brutalist, Bauhaus, Y2K.

## What each does better

Canva is better at scale: more templates, more stock, more people, more tools around the editor. OpenTempl is better at independence: it runs without a network, without an account and without a paid plan, and the exports are not held back. For one person making a poster, a post or a deck, that can be the whole decision. For a company with brand rules and ten people editing the same files, it cannot.

## FAQ

**Is OpenTempl better than Canva?**
For working offline, keeping your files and exporting without a paid plan, yes. For templates, stock, phone apps and teams, Canva is better.

**Can I move from Canva?**
Download a design from Canva as PDF or PowerPoint and open it in OpenTempl. Check the result, because fonts and effects may change. There is no direct import.

**Does OpenTempl have Canva's templates?**
No. It has its own {{templates}} templates, made for OpenTempl.

**Does OpenTempl copy Canva?**
No. It is a separate editor written from scratch.
