Design a menu, a cafe board or a recipe card in OpenTempl and export a print ready PDF with bleed and crop marks. No account, no watermark, and it works offline.

## Start from a menu

The [menus and food templates](/templates/food-menu) cover cafe boards, restaurant menus and recipe cards. Other styles work for a menu too: [quiet luxury](/templates/quiet-luxury) for a dinner, [Nordic minimal](/templates/nordic-minimal) for a coffee bar, [seventies](/templates/seventies) for a diner.

## Make it yours

1. Pick a template and a page size. A4 and A5 are common; type your own for a tall board.
2. Edit the dishes and prices. Paste a table from a spreadsheet to build a chart or a list, or use bulk create for one page per item, such as a table card per dish.
3. Set your brand colours and fonts with a brand kit.
4. Add a QR code that links to your site or an online menu.
5. Export the print PDF with bleed and crop marks, or a PNG for a screen.

## What it does not do

OpenTempl is a design tool. It does not host a QR menu for you, take orders or print. You export the file and take it to a print shop or your own printer. It works in RGB; ask your printer about CMYK.

## FAQ

**Can I add a QR code?**
Yes. Add a QR code element and type the address it should open.

**What size should a menu be?**
Common sizes are A4, A5 and a tall DL card. Ask your printer which they prefer.

**Can I print at a copy shop?**
Yes. Export the print PDF with bleed and crop marks and take it in.
