Make Instagram posts, stories and carousels in OpenTempl without an account or a watermark. Start from a template or a blank 4:5 post, 1:1 square or 9:16 story, and export a PNG or JPG at full size.

## Sizes

- Post, 4:5 portrait, 1080 by 1350 pixels
- Square post, 1:1, 1080 by 1080
- Story and reel cover, 9:16, 1080 by 1920

Instagram changes what it supports, so check its current specs. Magic resize turns a finished post into a story, or the other way round, in place or as a copy.

## Make a post

1. Pick a size and a style. Soft and quiet: [soft pastel](/templates/soft-pastel), [Nordic minimal](/templates/nordic-minimal), [quiet luxury](/templates/quiet-luxury). Loud: [Y2K chrome](/templates/y2k-chrome), [maximal pop](/templates/maximal-pop), [aura](/templates/gradient-aura).
2. Swap the words and the photo. Remove a background if the subject should sit on colour.
3. Save your colours, fonts and logo as a brand kit so every post matches.
4. Export a PNG or JPG.

## Carousels and series

A carousel is a design with several pages. Export a ZIP of all pages and upload them in order. For a series, such as one card per product, use bulk create: paste a table and get a page for every row.

## What it does not do

OpenTempl does not schedule or publish to Instagram. You export the image and post it yourself.

## FAQ

**What size is an Instagram post?**
A 4:5 portrait post is 1080 by 1350 pixels and a square post is 1080 by 1080. Check Instagram's current guidance, because it changes.

**Can I make a carousel?**
Yes. Add pages to a design and export a ZIP of all pages.

**Can I schedule posts?**
No. OpenTempl makes the image; you post it.

**Is there a watermark?**
No.
